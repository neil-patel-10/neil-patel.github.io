Surrogate-Optimized EV Battery Cooling Channel Geometry
M.S. THESIS · SAN JOSÉ STATE UNIVERSITY
IN PROGRESS

Running a Latin hypercube-sampled sweep of steady-state conjugate heat transfer simulations across channel width, height, and mass flow rate on a battery liquid-cooling unit cell. Training a neural network surrogate (target R² ≥ 0.95) to stand in for the CFD, then using NSGA-II to trace a Pareto front trading off maximum cell-wall temperature against coolant pressure drop.
